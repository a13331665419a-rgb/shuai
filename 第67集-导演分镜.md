## 一、视觉连续性锁定 / Continuity Locks（全场复用 · reuse across the scene）

### 角色 · 凯恩 / Kane
成年男性，挺拔高大，肩背宽厚，军人站姿。穿最高规格“战神统帅”常服，肩章缀将星，胸前佩戴守护者勋章（人类最后希望的象征）。
暗金色眼瞳，下颌线锋利，气场沉稳压制；情绪由王者锋芒转为注视伊芙时的温柔。
Adult male, tall and commanding, broad shoulders, military bearing. Highest-grade Warrior God's commander dress uniform, general's stars on the shoulders, Guardian medal on the chest. Dark-gold irises, sharp jaw, dominant calm; softens only when looking at Eve.

### 角色 · 伊芙 / Eve
成年女性，气质清冷高贵，城主身份。身披深蓝色城主披风，立领，质感厚重，随空调微风轻摆。
眼神由依靠的温柔转为眼眶微红的动情；嘴角能勾出惊艳的笑。
Adult female, cool and noble, the City Lord. Deep-blue City Lord cape, high collar, heavy fabric stirring in the air-conditioned breeze. Gaze shifts from leaning tenderness to red-rimmed emotion; able to lift a striking smile.

### 场景 · 穹顶指挥塔 / Dome Command Tower
灭世级母舰顶端的全透明穹顶指挥塔，高分子防弹玻璃环绕。突破千米暗蓝天灾屏障后，舱外是无辐射尘的纯净平流层与无垠星海。
全息人造阳光刺眼纯粹，透过玻璃斜洒舱内，形成强烈体积光与冷暖反差。
Fully glazed dome command tower atop the mothership, wrapped in polymer ballistic glass. Above the kilometer-high dark-blue calamity barrier: a clear, dust-free stratosphere and an endless star-sea. Pure artificial sunlight rakes through the glass as strong volumetric light.

### 关键物件 / Key Props
- 守护者勋章（暗金，胸前）— Guardian medal, dark gold, on the chest
- 肩章将星 — general's stars on the shoulders
- 深蓝城主披风 — deep-blue City Lord cape
